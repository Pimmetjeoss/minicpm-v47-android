Exclusions and publication review items
======================================
minja and sheredom/subprocess.h exist in the source tree but do not appear in
this native build's dependency records; their texts are not packaged merely
because they are vendored. Curl and OpenSSL are disabled and not inventoried
as bundled libraries. Conversion scripts, desktop-only dependencies and model
weights are outside this binary inventory.

No resolved runtime artifact is missing a license declaration or full text
in this inventory. This is not a legal clearance certificate.
Both upstream default reference WAV recordings are excluded from this APK
by the reproducible Android asset ignore pattern (*.wav and ref_audios).
Their provenance/speaker permission was not established: neither their bytes
nor binary patches are distributed here. TTS preset buttons are disabled and
an explicit unavailable warning is displayed. User-recorded/no-reference TTS
remains separate from MiniCPM-V 4.7 1B and untested. Do not redistribute a full
upstream checkout or re-enable these voice assets without a separate review.
